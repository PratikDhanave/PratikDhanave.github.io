Why AI pilots stall

It's almost never the model. Pilots die of ambiguity: success was never defined, the bar quietly moved, it ran on tidy data that doesn't represent the real workload, no one owns the day after it passes, or the trial just... never ends. The result is a "promising but inconclusive" limbo that burns time and credibility.

What the rescue does

  • Diagnose — find the real reason it's stuck (criteria, data, evaluation, cost, trust, or ownership).
  • Re-scope — set the production success criteria that should have been agreed up front: task, bar, sign-off, day-after.
  • Fix the crux — the specific blocker between the pilot and production, on real data.
  • Prove or call it — reach a defensible result against the bar — a clear win to ship, or an honest go/no-go so you stop sinking cost.

What you get

  • A clear diagnosis of why the pilot stalled, in writing.
  • Agreed production success criteria and a path to them.
  • Either a shippable, proven result — or a confident, evidence-backed decision to stop.

Scoped to the outcome. Often the fastest, highest-leverage way to unblock an AI investment that's gone quiet.

Who it's for

Teams with a funded AI pilot that's drifted past its deadline, and leaders who need a straight answer on whether it can ship.
