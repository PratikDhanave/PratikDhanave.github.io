The problem it solves

Your team built a demo that impressed everyone — and then it stalled. Real data is messy, cost at volume is scary, security review is looming, and the edge cases keep multiplying. The hard 80% of an AI product lives after the demo, and it's hard to cross alone. A fractional forward deployed engineer closes that gap by building the win *with* you, in your environment.

What you get

  • A senior engineer embedded with your team (part-time / fractional), building on your real data from day one.
  • A first production win shipped end to end — not a demo, a system that clears a bar you agreed up front.
  • Evaluation, guardrails, cost/latency control, and a security posture that survives review.
  • A clean handover — code, architecture, and the understanding behind it — so your team runs it without me.
  • A reusable pattern left behind, so the next win is cheaper.

How it works — the Deploy-to-Win Loop

  1. Qualify & Scope — agree the production success criteria (task, bar, sign-off) before building.
  2. Build — the thinnest thing that proves value on your real data.
  3. Prove — measured against the agreed bar, with evaluation and guardrails.
  4. Productize & hand over — harden, govern, document, and transfer ownership.

More on the method in the Forward-Deployed Playbook and the AI Forward Deployed Engineer series.

AI and technical teams — from seed-stage startups to enterprises and regulated banks — with a promising demo that has to become dependable production value. I've delivered across FinTech, Open Banking, telecom, energy, and critical national infrastructure.
