How these fit together

The four offers are one ladder, ordered by how deeply I get involved. A focused session answers a specific question in an afternoon — an architecture review, a security pass, a cost workshop. The audit gives you an evidence-based picture of where your AI system actually stands, and a prioritized plan to close the gaps. The rescue takes one stalled proof-of-concept and drives it to production — or to an honest go/no-go, which is sometimes the more valuable answer. And the fractional engagement embeds me with your team until the win is shipped, hardened, and owned by you — forward-deployed work in the full sense.

If you're unsure where to start, start small: an audit or a single session is usually how the bigger engagements begin. The approach behind all of them is the same one I've used from seed-stage startups to regulated enterprises — prove value on your real data first, then make it survive what a demo never meets: the security review, the cost at volume, the messy edge cases. It's the discipline behind a 57% cloud-cost reduction at Tata and a 30K+ TPS payment engine at Globe, and it's written up in the free Forward-Deployed Playbook if you'd like to see the method before we talk.
