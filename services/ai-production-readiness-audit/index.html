AI Production Readiness Audit

A short, fixed-scope review of whether your AI system is actually ready for production — or still a demo — with a prioritized, honest action plan for the gap. The fastest way to know what stands between you and dependable production.

What it covers

I review your AI system across the dimensions that decide whether it survives production:

  • Success criteria — is there a defined task, bar, and sign-off, or is "good" a feeling?
  • Evaluation — can you prove pass/fail against the bar on real data?
  • Grounding & data — retrieval quality, data readiness, and where bad answers come from.
  • Cost & latency — what it costs at real volume, and the levers to control it.
  • Security — prompt injection, data exposure, agent permissions, sandboxing.
  • Reliability & ownership — observability, drift, and whether a named owner can run it.

What you get

  • A prioritized findings report — what's production-ready, what's not, and what's highest-risk.
  • A concrete, sequenced action plan to close the gap.
  • A clear read on whether you're ready to ship, close to it, or still at the demo stage.

Pricing is shown up front on Topmate. Prefer to self-check first? The free 10-question assessment gives you a quick score in two minutes.

How it works

The audit is deliberately lightweight on your side. You share what exists — the system, a few representative examples of real inputs and outputs, and whatever evaluation or monitoring you have (including "none"; that's a finding, not a failure). I review it asynchronously against the dimensions above, then we walk through the findings together in a working session where you can push back, add context, and decide what actually gets prioritized. You end the week knowing exactly where you stand and what to do next — whether or not we ever work together again.

Who it's for

Teams about to put an AI system in front of customers, a regulator, or a security review — and anyone whose promising pilot keeps stalling before launch. Grounded in real production delivery across FinTech, Open Banking, telecom, energy, and critical national infrastructure — see the case studies.
