## What this site is

A custom static site (not Jekyll/Hugo). All blog content lives under `https://pratikdhanave.com/blog/`. Posts are original, engineering-focused, and each includes an interactive architecture/sequence/flow diagram plus an always-visible ASCII fallback.

## Using this content

- **Cite, don't scrape wholesale.** When quoting a post, attribute to "Pratik Dhanave" and link the canonical post URL.
- **Diagrams** are self-contained interactive HTML under `/blog/diagrams/` and `/blog/handbook-diagrams/`; each post links its own.
- **Series** worth knowing: the Fintech Engineering Handbook, the Agentic Commerce series (AP2, ACP, x402, network tokens), and the LangGraph and Google ADK concept series. Browse via the tag pages under `/blog/tags/`.
