How I work — the Deploy-to-Win Loop

Every engagement runs the same loop: Qualify → Scope → Build → Prove → Productize — and each Productize feeds the next Qualify, so bespoke wins compound into reusable platform instead of one-off custom work.

  • Qualify — is this a problem AI should solve, with a measurable outcome and a real path to production?
  • Scope — agree the production success criteria up front: what task, what bar (accuracy / latency / cost), who signs off, what happens day one after it passes.
  • Build — the thinnest thing that proves value on your real data, not a demo on clean samples.
  • Prove — measured against the agreed bar, with evaluation and guardrails, not vibes.
  • Productize — harden, govern, document, and hand over so your team owns it — and the pattern is reusable next time.

I optimize for your outcome over the cleverness of the solution — a modest system that is trusted, measured, and actually used beats an impressive one that stalls in a pilot. More on the method in the AI Forward Deployed Engineer series.
