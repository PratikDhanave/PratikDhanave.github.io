Caching at the Gateway

Model calls are slow and expensive, and a surprising fraction of them are repeats or near-repeats. Caching at the gateway turns those into instant, free responses — and because the gateway sees all traffic, it's the one place a cache benefits every application at once. Beyond exact-match caching, semantic caching catches queries that mean the same thing in different words, which is where the real savings live.

Routing and Load Balancing

Together they turn the gateway from a passthrough into a control plane.
