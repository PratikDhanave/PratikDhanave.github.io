Model Evolution II: Test-Time Training and the Continual Learning Problem

Post 5 asked where the training signal comes from; this post asks when the weights change — and the two most radical answers bracket the question. Test-time training updates weights during inference, per input, then throws the update away; continual learning updates weights forever and keeps them. One treats adaptation as disposable, the other as permanent — and between them sits the oldest unsolved problem in neural networks: learning the new without trampling the old.

Test-time training adapts weights per input and discards the update — the deepest locus at the narrowest closure; continual learning keeps updates and collides with catastrophic interference, managed by replay, regularization, and isolation. Underneath both: the stability–plasticity dilemma and the two-timescale resolution that meets the memory series.
