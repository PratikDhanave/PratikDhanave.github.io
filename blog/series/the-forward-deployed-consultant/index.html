The Forward Deployed Consultant

The craft and business of forward-deployed consulting — a first-person, practitioner series on building a consulting practice around the forward deployed engineer model (embedding with a team to ship a real result on their real data, rather than handing over a deck or renting hours). What forward-deployed consulting is and how it differs from strategy consulting and staff augmentation; finding the first client (trust that already exists, not a funnel); scoping the first engagement to a bounded first win; pricing the outcome instead of hours (value-based pricing as a trust signal); the discovery phase (learning how the work really happens, what the data is really like, and what ‘good enough to trust’ means); earning trust in the first two weeks (small visible usefulness, credibility plus warmth, winning over the skeptic); saying no as the underrated consulting skill; building alongside the team rather than in a black box (knowledge transfer, pairing); the handoff (engineering your own obsolescence, handing over understanding not just artifacts); turning one win into a practice (referrals and repeat work that compound); staying independent and sustainable (avoiding burnout, financial and psychological independence); and the long game (reputation as the only durable asset, generosity as strategy, what stays when the technology changes). A companion to the technical [Forward Deployed Engineering] and [The AI Forward Deployed Engineer] series, focused on the practice itself and anchored in the multiplier mindset. Lightly grounded in references (Palantir, management consulting, value-based pricing, knowledge transfer, referral/word-of-mouth marketing, occupational burnout, reputation, Liz Wiseman’s Multipliers).
