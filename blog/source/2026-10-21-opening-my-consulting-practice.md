# Helping AI Teams Turn Great Demos into Real Production Value

*I'm opening up my consulting, focused on forward deployed engineering for AI and technical teams. Here's the work I'd love to help with — and why it matters to me.*

One of the most remarkable things about this moment in AI is how quickly a good idea can become a convincing demo. In an afternoon, a team can build something that genuinely impresses a customer. That capability would have felt like magic just a few years ago.

The next step is where some of the most meaningful work lives: turning that promising demo into something that delivers real, dependable value inside a customer's actual environment. It asks for care — working with real data, keeping an eye on cost, earning trust, and handling the small details and edge cases that decide whether a system is genuinely useful.

Helping teams make that journey well is work I find deeply rewarding. It's what I'm opening up my consulting practice to focus on, and I wanted to share why.

## A way of working I've come to admire

There's a name for this approach: the Forward Deployed Engineer. The idea, pioneered at Palantir and now embraced widely across the AI world, is simple and very human. Instead of handing over software and hoping it fits, an engineer works alongside the customer — learning their world, understanding their real constraints, and building the solution together with them.

The more I've practiced and studied this way of working, the more I've come to value it. A frontier model is an incredible capability, but it becomes truly valuable only when it's connected to a specific team's data, workflows, and goals. That connecting work — done thoughtfully, in partnership with the customer — is where I love to spend my time.

## A simple way to think about it

As I've gone deeper, I've found it helpful to see forward deployment in three layers: building the first real win, making that win repeatable and well-architected, and running it as a healthy, sustainable practice. I've written about this in more depth as [The Forward Deployment Stack](/blog/series/the-forward-deployment-stack/), if you'd like the fuller picture.

And however I help, I try to hold to one standard above the others — [the multiplier mindset](/blog/posts/consulting-multiplier-mindset.html): leaving a team genuinely stronger than I found it, rather than creating a dependency on me.

## How I hope to help

I come to this as a builder first, and I'd rather help prove value by building something real in your environment than by talking about it in the abstract. A few of the ways I can support teams:

- **Embedding to help build a first production win** on your real data, end to end.
- **Helping turn early one-off successes** into a reusable, well-architected foundation the whole team can build on.
- **Supporting founders and leaders** as they thoughtfully stand up a forward-deployment practice — how to begin, who to involve, and what to measure.
- **Coaching engineers** who want to grow into confident, customer-facing work.

## Who I'd love to work with

AI and technical teams who have promising demos and want a hand bringing them dependably into production — founders taking their first technical product to market, and sales-engineering or solutions leaders building a modern, customer-centered go-to-market approach.
